01 / Sabse pehle, humari dosti

Our friendship
means so much.

Sabse pehle main tumhe ye batana chahta hoon ki tum meri life mein kitni important rahi ho. Maine honestly kabhi nahi socha tha ki meri life mein koi aisa insaan aayega jiske saath main itni achhi aur itni gehri friendship feel karunga. Main pehle friendship ko itna seriously ya itni depth mein samajhta bhi nahi tha. Apni baatein share karna, kisi ko apne din ka hissa banana, kisi ke message ka wait karna — ye sab mere liye itna natural nahi tha.

Lekin dheere-dheere tum meri daily life ka ek bahut achha hissa ban gayi. Kabhi random si baat, kabhi bina kisi reason ke hasi-mazaak, kabhi serious conversations, kabhi ek doosre ko samjhana — ye chhoti-chhoti cheezein mere liye bahut matter karne lagi. Ho sakta hai tumhe un sab moments ki value ka andaaza na ho, lekin mere liye woh moments genuinely special the.

Tumne mujhe ye feel karaya ki dosti sirf saath baat karne ya time spend karne ka naam nahi hoti. Dosti mein care hoti hai, patience hota hai, ek doosre ki baat sunna hota hai, aur kabhi-kabhi bina kahe bhi samajhne ki koshish hoti hai. I started believing in genuine friendship a little more because of you.

Aur shayad isi wajah se aaj mujhe apni galtiyon ka itna ehsaas ho raha hai. Kyunki jis dosti ko main itna special maanta tha, usi dosti ko main apne actions se utni care nahi de paaya. Tum meri life mein ek bahut achhe dost ke roop mein aayi, aur main dil se thankful hoon ki humari mulaqat hui.

02 / Apni galtiyon ko maanna

The things I got wrong.

Is baar koi safai nahi. Bas jo galat kiya, use honestly accept karna hai.

01

Main tumhe samajh nahi paaya

Bahut baar tumne mujhe samjhane ki koshish ki. Tum apni taraf se baat clear karna chahti thi, apna perspective batana chahti thi, lekin main kai baar tumhari baat ko poora sunne aur samajhne se pehle hi apne thoughts mein chala jaata tha. Main ye sochta raha ki mujhe kya feel ho raha hai, mujhe tumhari baat kaisi lag rahi hai, lekin maine hamesha utni imaandari se ye nahi socha ki tum kya kehna chahti ho aur tum kis feeling se woh baat keh rahi ho. Tumhe baar-baar apni baat samjhani padi, aur ye tumhare liye kitna tiring raha hoga, main ab samajh sakta hoon. Mujhe tumhari baat ko defend karne ya oppose karne ke liye nahi, samajhne ke liye sunna chahiye tha.

02

Maine apne words se tumhe hurt kiya

Kai baar maine bina soche-samjhe aisi baatein bol di jo kisi bhi dost ko nahi bolni chahiye. Gusse mein ya hurt feel karte hue maine apne words par control nahi rakha. Tumhe achhi friend na kehna, tumhari friendship par sawaal uthana, ya tumhare efforts ko chhota feel karana — ye sab galat tha. Main jaanta hoon ki baat keh dene ke baad use wapas nahi liya ja sakta. Ho sakta hai mere liye woh kuch pal ka gussa raha ho, lekin tumhare liye woh words bahut der tak dard ban kar rahe hon. Tumne mujh par trust karke friendship rakhi, aur meri taraf se tumhe aise words sunne pade. Iske liye main genuinely sharminda hoon.

03

Maine tumhari care aur efforts ki value nahi ki

Tumne meri kitni baatein suni, kitni baar mujhe samjhaya, kitni baar meri galtiyon ke baad bhi baat ko sambhalne ki koshish ki. Tumne apni taraf se friendship ko importance di. Lekin main kai baar tumhari patience ko granted leta raha. Main sochta raha ki tum samajh jaogi, tum maan jaogi, main sorry bol dunga toh baat theek ho jayegi. Lekin maine ye nahi samjha ki har baar sorry bolne se kisi ka hurt khatam nahi hota. Agar main baar-baar wahi galti karta rahoon, toh meri apology ka matlab kamzor pad jaata hai. Tumhe sirf sorry nahi, mere behaviour mein care, respect aur change dikhna chahiye tha. Main woh consistency nahi de paaya.

04

Main sirf apna perspective dekhta raha

Main apni feelings ko itni importance deta raha ki kabhi-kabhi tumhari feelings ko dekhna hi bhool gaya. Main chahta tha ki tum mujhe samjho, meri baat samjho, mere reaction ke peeche ki wajah samjho. Lekin maine utni baar ruk kar nahi socha ki meri baaton se tumhe kaisa lag raha hai. Tum bhi hurt hoti ho, tum bhi overthink kar sakti ho, tumhara bhi mental peace important hai, aur tumhe bhi kabhi kisi aise dost ki zaroorat hoti hai jo bina argue kiye tumhari baat sun le. Friendship ek taraf se understanding maangne ka naam nahi hai. Mujhe bhi utna hi effort karna chahiye tha.

03 / Kuch din pehle jo hua

That conversation
was my mistake too.

Jo kuch din pehle humare beech hua, main us baat ko ignore nahi karna chahta. Us waqt tumhari kuch baaton se mujhe hurt feel hua tha. Lekin mujhe apni feelings ko samajhkar calmly baat karni chahiye thi. Uske bajaye maine un feelings ko apne words par haavi hone diya. Tum apni taraf se mujhe samjhane ki koshish kar rahi thi, lekin main apni baat bolta hi gaya.

Maine tumse aisi baatein keh di jo mujhe kabhi nahi kehni chahiye thi. Maine tumhe achhi friend na hone jaisi baat keh di, jabki sach ye hai ki tumne meri life mein ek achhe dost ki tarah bahut kuch kiya hai. Main ek moment mein hurt tha, lekin us moment ke hurt mein maine tumhari poori friendship aur tumhare saare efforts ko unfairly judge kar diya. Ye bilkul theek nahi tha.

Mujhe ye bhi yaad hai ki meri baaton se tum royi thi. Aur ye sochkar mujhe bura lagta hai ki jis insaan ne mujhe itni baar samjhane ki koshish ki, use meri wajah se itna hurt feel hua. Lekin main ye nahi chahta ki mera guilt tum par koi extra burden bane. Tumhara hurt apni jagah real hai, aur meri responsibility hai ki main apni galti ko bina excuse ke accept karoon.

Mujhe us waqt ruk jaana chahiye tha. Tumhari baat poori sunni chahiye thi. Agar main disagree bhi karta, toh respect ke saath baat kar sakta tha. Being hurt doesn't give me the right to hurt you.

“Mujhe tumhe galat samajhne se pehle tumhari baat samajhne ki koshish karni chahiye thi.”

04 / Tum meri life mein kya ho

You are irreplaceable.

Tum meri life ke sabse special friends mein se ho. Main ye baat sirf isliye nahi keh raha kyunki aaj mujhe guilt feel ho raha hai. Ye woh baat hai jo main pehle bhi feel karta tha, bas shayad tumhe utni achhi tarah dikha nahi paaya. Tumhari jagah meri life mein koi random insaan nahi le sakta. Tumhare jaisa dost dobara milega ya nahi, main nahi jaanta, lekin tumhare saath jo bond bana, woh mere liye apni jagah alag hai.

Aur isi baat ka dukh bhi hai ki jis insaan ko main itna important maanta hoon, usi insaan ko main baar-baar hurt karta raha. Main tumhari friendship deserve karta hoon ya nahi, iska faisla tumhara hai. Lekin apni taraf se main itna maanta hoon ki tumne jitni achhi friendship di, main utna achha friend ban kar uski care nahi kar paaya. Tumne jitni patience, concern aur understanding dikhayi, main utna return nahi kar saka.

Main apne aap ko tumse better friend nahi keh sakta. Sach toh ye hai ki tumne mujhe dosti ka jo meaning samjhaya, main khud us par utna achha utar nahi paaya. I will always be grateful that you became a part of my life. Tumhari importance meri life mein kisi comparison ya replacement se define nahi hoti.

You taught me more than you know.

Tumne mujhe genuine friendship par bharosa karna sikhaya.

✧

Tumne dikhaya ki care sirf words nahi, chhoti-chhoti efforts mein bhi hoti hai.

☼

Tumne samjhaya ki har insaan ka perspective aur feelings important hoti hain.

☆

Tumne mujhe yaad dilaya ki kisi ki patience aur kindness ko granted nahi lena chahiye.

Shayad main tumhari tarah achha dost nahi ban paaya, lekin tumne mujhe dikhaya ki ek achhi friendship kaisi hoti hai.

Tumne meri life mein sirf happiness nahi add ki, balki mujhe apne baare mein sochne ka ek naya tareeka bhi diya. Kabhi tumhari kisi baat se, kabhi tumhare samjhane se, aur kabhi tumhare patiently sunne se maine bahut kuch seekha. Ho sakta hai main har lesson ko waqt par apply nahi kar paaya, lekin iska matlab ye nahi ki tumhari baatein mere liye matter nahi karti thi. I remember the good things you brought into my life, and I am thankful for them.

06 / Dil se ek maafi

One sincere sorry.

Mujhe sach mein nahi pata ki itni saari feelings ko shabdon mein kaise samjhaun. Kaafi din se mere mann mein bahut kuch chal raha tha. Main normal kaam kar raha hota tha, padhne baithta tha, khana kha raha hota tha ya kisi aur cheez mein busy hota tha, lekin beech-beech mein wahi baat dimaag mein wapas aa jaati thi — ki maine tumse aisa kyun kaha, maine tumhe itna hurt kyun kiya, aur maine us insaan ke saath aisa kaise kar diya jisne meri itni care ki.

Kai baar raat ko bhi woh conversation yaad aati thi. Main sochta rehta tha ki tumne meri baaton ko kaise liya hoga, tumhe kitna bura laga hoga, aur shayad tum un words ko abhi tak yaad kar rahi hogi. Mujhe apni neend aur apne kaam par bhi dhyaan lagana mushkil lag raha tha. Mere exams bhi paas aa rahe hain, padhai karni thi, apne kaam karne the, lekin mera mind baar-baar usi guilt par atak jaata tha. Main ye sab tumhe guilty feel karane ke liye nahi keh raha, aur na hi tumhe meri mental state sambhalne ki responsibility dena chahta hoon. Main bas itna bata raha hoon ki mujhe apni galti ka ehsaas kitni gehraai se ho raha tha.

Sabse zyada takleef is baat ki thi ki meri wajah se tumhe bhi apna time aur mental peace us situation ke baare mein sochne mein lagana pada hoga. Tumhari apni life hai, tumhari padhai hai, tumhare apne kaam hain, tumhari apni feelings aur responsibilities hain. Tumhara waqt un cheezon par jaana chahiye tha jo tumhare liye important hain, na ki meri kahi hui hurtful baaton ko baar-baar yaad karke pareshaan hone mein. Main nahi jaanta ki tum par iska kitna asar hua, lekin mujhe fikr hoti hai ki meri wajah se tumhe extra stress, overthinking ya sadness face karni padi hogi. Tum deserve nahi karti thi ki meri galti ka bojh tum apne saath lekar chalo.

Mujhe yaad aata hai ki tumne kitni baar mujhe samjhaya. Tumne apni taraf se baat clear karne ki koshish ki, meri baat suni, meri mistakes ke baad bhi mujhe maaf kiya aur friendship ko sambhalne ki koshish ki. Aur main? Main sorry bolkar aage badhta raha, lekin kai baar apne behaviour ko waise change nahi kar paaya jaise karna chahiye tha. Ab samajh aata hai ki baar-baar kisi ko hurt karna aur phir ek sorry keh dena kaafi nahi hota. Maafi mil jaana ye proof nahi hota ki saamne wale ka hurt khatam ho gaya. Mujhe tumhari forgiveness ko ek aur chance samajhkar apne actions sudharne chahiye the, na ki use automatically milne wali cheez samajhna chahiye tha.

Mujhe lagta hai maine tumhari friendship ko utni care nahi di jitni woh deserve karti thi. Tumne jis patience se mujhe samjha, jis tarah meri baaton ko suna, jis tarah chhoti-chhoti cheezon mein care dikhayi, main uska aadha bhi consistently return nahi kar paaya. Main apni feelings ko samajhne aur express karne mein itna busy raha ki tumhari feelings ko utni jagah hi nahi di. Tum meri achhi friend thi, lekin main tumhare liye utna achha friend nahi ban paaya jitna mujhe banna chahiye tha. Is baat ko accept karna mere liye aasaan nahi hai, lekin ye meri responsibility hai ki main isse nazar na churaun.

Jo maine kuch din pehle kaha, woh galat tha. Tumhe bad friend kehna, tumhari friendship aur efforts par sawaal uthana, aur aise words bolna jisse tumhe lage ki tumne mere liye jo kuch kiya uski koi value nahi — ye sab meri taraf se unfair tha. Main us waqt hurt tha, lekin main apne hurt ko tumhe hurt karne ka reason nahi bana sakta. Mujhe rukna chahiye tha, tumhari baat poori sunni chahiye thi, aur agar mujhe kuch bura laga bhi tha toh calmly kehna chahiye tha. Tumhe rulana ya tumhe apni friendship par doubt karwana kabhi bhi theek nahi tha.

Main ye bhi maanta hoon ki shayad maine pehle bhi tumhe kai baar hurt kiya hai. Ho sakta hai har baar mujhe laga ho ki baat chhoti thi, ya sorry kehne ke baad sab normal ho jayega. Lekin tumhare liye woh chhoti baat nahi rahi hogi. Har baar jab tumhe meri baaton se bura laga, har baar jab tumhe laga ki main tumhe samajh nahi raha, aur har baar jab tumhe apni feelings explain karni padi — mujhe us sab ko seriously lena chahiye tha. I should have paid attention before things reached this point.

Kabhi-kabhi mujhe lagta hai ki main tumhari itni achhi friendship deserve karne jaisa dost ban hi nahi paaya. Tumne mujhe ek genuine friend ki tarah care di, lekin main tumhe wahi safety, patience aur understanding consistently nahi de saka. Main khud ko neecha dikhane ke liye ye nahi keh raha; main bas ye maan raha hoon ki tumne jo friendship di, main uski responsibility achhe se nahi nibha paaya. Tumhari kindness aur forgiveness ko lightly lena meri galti thi.

Ek baat main bilkul clear rakhna chahta hoon: main is letter ke badle tumse kuch bhi expect nahi kar raha. Main ye nahi keh raha ki tum mujhe maaf kar do. Main ye bhi nahi keh raha ki tum mujhse baat karna shuru karo, pehle jaisi friendship wapas le aao, ya mujhe ek aur chance do. Tum maaf karna chahti ho ya nahi, baat karna chahti ho ya nahi, future mein is friendship ko kis tarah dekhna chahti ho — ye poori tarah tumhara decision hai. Agar tum mujhe maaf nahi karna chahti, toh bhi main tumhare decision ko respect karunga. Tum par koi obligation nahi hai ki meri apology accept karo.

Main tumhe ye letter isliye nahi bhej raha ki tum mere guilt ko kam karo, mujhe reassure karo, ya mujhe batao ki sab theek hai. Main nahi chahta ki tumhe lage ki ab tumhe mere liye kuch kehna ya karna zaroori hai. Mere guilt ki responsibility meri hai. Main bas itne din se jo baat apne andar rakhe hue tha, use imaandari se kehna chahta tha. Mujhe laga ki tumse maafi maangna zaroori hai, kyunki maine tumhe hurt kiya aur tum is sach ko jaane bina reh jao ki mujhe apni galti ka ehsaas hai — ye mujhe theek nahi laga.

Ho sakta hai meri ye baatein padhkar bhi tumhara hurt kam na ho. Ho sakta hai tum abhi bhi gusse mein ho, disappointed ho, ya tumhe mujhse distance chahiye. Main in sab feelings ko galat nahi kahunga. Tumhe jo feel ho raha hai, uska haq tumhe hai. Main bas itna chahta hoon ki meri taraf se tum tak ye baat pahunch jaaye ki main apne words aur actions ko galat maanta hoon, aur main unke liye dil se maafi maangta hoon.

Tumhe jitni baar hurt kiya, tumhari feelings ko jitni baar samajhne mein fail hua, tumhari care aur friendship ki jitni baar value nahi kar paaya — un sab ke liye dil se sorry. Main tumse forgiveness demand nahi kar raha. Bas apni galti accept kar raha hoon, aur jo mere dil mein itne din se tha woh tumhe bata raha hoon.— With a sincere heart

07 / Ek thank you jo kehna zaroori hai

Thank you for
being in my life.

Ab end karne se pehle main tumhe sirf sorry nahi, thank you bhi kehna chahta hoon. Thank you meri life mein aane ke liye. Thank you mujhe apna dost samajhne ke liye. Thank you un saari chhoti-badi cheezon ke liye jo tumne mere liye ki, chahe maine har baar unhe notice kiya ho ya nahi.

Thank you meri random baatein sunne ke liye, mujhe samjhane ke liye, meri care karne ke liye, aur un moments ke liye jab tumhari ek simple si baat se mera din achha ho gaya. Tumne mujhe jitni happiness di, jitna comfortable feel karaya, aur jitni achhi memories di, un sab ke liye main genuinely grateful hoon.

Mujhe afsos hai ki main tumhe utna appreciate nahi kar paaya jitna karna chahiye tha. Kaash main tumhari efforts ko tabhi samajh paata jab tum mere saath thi aur mujhe samjha rahi thi. I hope you know that your kindness and friendship mattered to me, even when my actions failed to show it.

Tum meri life ka ek bahut achha hissa rahi ho. Is baat ke liye main hamesha thankful rahunga. Thank you for being you. ❤️

08 / Aakhri kuch baatein

A few last words...

Mujhe nahi pata ki ab humari friendship kis jagah par hai. Shayad pehle jaisi baatein na hon, shayad ab kuch cheezein badal gayi hon, aur shayad unhe pehle jaisa hone mein waqt lage — ya woh kabhi pehle jaisi na ho. Main is possibility ko bhi samajhne ki koshish kar raha hoon.

Main tumse kuch maang nahi raha. Na reply, na forgiveness, na koi promise. Tumhe jo sahi lage, jo tumhare liye peaceful aur comfortable ho, wahi karna. Main tumhare decision ko respect karunga.

Bas meri ek chhoti si wish hai: kabhi future mein humari dosti yaad aaye, toh sirf woh hurtful baatein hi yaad na rahein. Agar tumhe theek lage, toh un achhe moments ko bhi yaad kar lena jo humne share kiye the. Lekin tum par aisa karne ka bhi koi pressure nahi hai.

Apna bahut dhyan rakhna. Khush rehna, apni care karna, aur kabhi apni value kam mat samajhna. Tum care, respect aur understanding deserve karti ho — har friendship mein.

Main dil se sorry hoon. Aur meri life ka hissa banne ke liye, dil se thank you. ♥
